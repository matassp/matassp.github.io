## Introduction

Radija ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our radio streaming application handles information when you use our service.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

Radija is designed with privacy in mind. The app:
- Does not require account creation or login
- Does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
- Does not access your device's contacts, photos, or location
- Does not track your identity or personal data

## Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

### Recently Played Stations
- A local history of radio stations you've played
- Stored only on your device
- Never transmitted to our servers or any third party

### Favorite Stations
- A list of stations you've marked as favorites
- Stored only on your device
- Never transmitted to our servers or any third party

### App Settings
- Your preferences and app configuration
- Stored only on your device

**Important:** All of this data remains on your device and is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to us or any third party.

## Radio Streaming

When you play a radio station:
- The app connects directly to the radio station's streaming server
- We do not intermediate or monitor these connections
- The radio station may have its own privacy practices
- We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of individual radio stations if you have concerns about their data practices

## Station Information

The app retrieves a list of available radio stations from our server (hosted on GitHub Pages). This list includes:
- Station names
- Stream URLs
- Station rankings

This request does not include any personal information or identifiers about you.

## Third-Party Services

### Radio Station Streams
When you play a radio station, you connect directly to that station's streaming service. We do not control or have access to any data collected by these third-party services.

### Apple Services
As an iOS app, Radija uses standard Apple frameworks for audio playback. Apple's privacy policy applies to their services.

## Children's Privacy

Radija does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else. The app is suitable for all ages as it simply provides access to publicly available radio streams.

## Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit your personal data, there is no risk to your privacy. All data stored by the app remains on your device and is protected by your device's security features.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this location with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect your personal information, there is no data for you to access, modify, or delete. You can delete all locally stored data (favorites and history) by uninstalling the app from your device.
